# Privacy

This is a single-page reference site. Nothing is sold, there are no ads, and there
is no sign-up. The only data collection is light-touch Google Analytics, and only
if you say yes.

## What is collected

If you accept the cookie banner, Google Analytics 4 records:

- Page views
- Where you came from (referrer)
- Approximate location (country / region — derived from your IP, which is then discarded)
- Device class and browser

It does **not** record your name, email, exact IP address, or anything that identifies
you personally. There are no advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking, no
fingerprinting.

If you reject the banner, none of this happens — no cookies are set and no data leaves
your browser. The banner remembers your choice in `localStorage` so you aren't asked
again.

## Who processes it

[Google LLC](https://policies.google.com/privacy) processes the data on our behalf as
the GA4 provider. IP anonymisation is enabled and ad-related signals are disabled.
Data is retained for 14 months (GA4 default) and then deleted automatically. This site
uses its own analytics property, separate from
[jasonduffett.net](https://jasonduffett.net).

## Changing your mind

You can withdraw consent any time using the **cookies** link in the footer. That
re-opens the banner and lets you switch your choice.

## Contact

Questions or want your data removed? Get in touch via the email on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/laazyj) linked from jasonduffett.net.
