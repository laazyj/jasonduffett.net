Jimmy Webb's _Wichita Lineman_ is a three-minute masterclass in longing. The chord changes do most of the work — all I've tried to do is get out of their way.

I've arranged it for a soprano uke in **low-G**. Standard tuning works, but the low string gives the verses somewhere to sit.

## The shape of it

- **Verse:** F — C/E — Dm — Bb — F — C — F
- **Refrain:** Bb — F — Gm — C — F

Play the verse fingerstyle, thumb on the low string, fingers walking through the top three. When the refrain arrives, let the strum open up — but only just.

## Notes

- The signature line ("_and I need you more than want you_") sits on the B♭. Hold it a beat longer than feels right.
- The string-bend that Glen Campbell plays at the end of each verse translates surprisingly well to a pull-off on the A-string, fret 3 → fret 1.
- Don't try to sing it at Campbell's key. Capo up two frets and give your throat a fighting chance.
